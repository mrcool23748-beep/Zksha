01 / THE BIG IDEA

What are humours?

In classical medical traditions, “humours” referred to bodily fluids thought to influence health and temperament.

✳

A historical framework

The theory of four humours was used for centuries to explain health, illness, and differences between people. It is important as a historical concept, not as a modern scientific explanation of disease.

A
CORE CONCEPT

Balance & imbalance

The traditional model suggested that wellbeing depended on the proper balance of the humours, while imbalance was believed to contribute to illness.
